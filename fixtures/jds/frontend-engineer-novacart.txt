Senior Frontend Engineer — NovaCart

NovaCart is looking for a Senior Frontend Engineer to help us reimagine our
storefront experience. You'll work closely with design, backend, and product
to ship features that millions of shoppers see every day.

What you'll do
- Build new features in React and Next.js across the storefront and account areas.
- Partner with our design system team on the next major version of our component library.
- Champion accessibility, performance, and code quality across the frontend codebase.
- Mentor other engineers through code review and pairing.

Must have
- 4+ years of professional frontend engineering experience.
- Deep proficiency with React and modern TypeScript.
- Experience shipping production Next.js applications.
- Strong understanding of web performance fundamentals (Core Web Vitals, bundle size, runtime profiling).
- Demonstrated commitment to web accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA or better).

Nice to have
- Experience working in a design system or shared component library.
- Familiarity with Tailwind CSS and class-variance-authority.
- E2E testing experience with Playwright or Cypress.
- Background in e-commerce, marketplaces, or consumer products is ideal.
- Familiarity with Storybook and visual regression testing would be a plus.

Soft skills we value
- Strong written communication; you can explain a tradeoff in a Slack message.
- Comfort owning ambiguous problems end to end.
- A bias toward shipping iteratively over polishing in private.

Logistics
Remote within the US, or hybrid out of our Seattle, WA office. No relocation
assistance offered. Must be eligible to work in the United States without
sponsorship.
