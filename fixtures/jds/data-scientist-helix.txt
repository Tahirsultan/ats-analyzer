Senior Data Scientist (Forecasting) — Helix Analytics

Helix Analytics is hiring a Senior Data Scientist with deep forecasting and
time-series expertise. You'll own demand and revenue forecasting models that
inform planning across the company.

Required
- 5+ years of professional data science experience, with at least 2 years focused on forecasting or time series.
- Expert-level Python (pandas, scikit-learn, statsmodels) and SQL.
- Hands-on experience with classical time-series methods (ARIMA, ETS, Prophet) and modern approaches (gradient-boosted models, deep-learning sequence models).
- Strong causal inference and experimentation background; proven track record running rigorous A/B tests.
- M.S. or Ph.D. in Statistics, Computer Science, Economics, or a quantitative field is required.
- AWS Certified Machine Learning – Specialty or equivalent cloud ML certification is required prior to start.
- Must be authorized to work in the United States; this role is not eligible for sponsorship.

Preferred
- Experience deploying models to production with MLflow, SageMaker, or an in-house platform.
- Familiarity with dbt and modern data warehouses (Snowflake or BigQuery).
- Prior work with hierarchical or grouped forecasting at SKU or store level.
- Experience presenting to executive stakeholders.

Responsibilities
- Build, evaluate, and operate forecasting models for revenue, demand, and capacity planning.
- Partner with finance and operations to integrate forecasts into planning cycles.
- Run experiments that change how the company invests across channels.
- Establish standards for model evaluation, monitoring, and retraining.

Travel
Up to 15% travel to our HQ in Austin, TX, for quarterly planning offsites.

Education
Required: M.S. or Ph.D. in a quantitative field.
