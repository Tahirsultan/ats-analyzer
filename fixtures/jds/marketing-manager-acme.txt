Marketing Manager — Acme Software

About the Role
We are hiring a Marketing Manager to lead demand generation and brand strategy for our B2B SaaS platform. You will own the marketing roadmap, manage a team of three, and partner with sales to grow the qualified pipeline.

Responsibilities
- Develop and execute the annual marketing strategy aligned with revenue goals.
- Lead demand generation campaigns across email, paid search, and content marketing.
- Manage the marketing budget and report on ROI to the executive team.
- Oversee SEO and content marketing to grow organic traffic.
- Partner with the sales team to define lead qualification criteria and improve conversion.
- Mentor and develop a team of three marketing specialists.

Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business, or a related field.
- 5+ years of B2B marketing experience.
- Proven experience managing a marketing budget of at least $500K.
- Strong analytical skills with experience in HubSpot and Google Analytics.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills.

Preferred Qualifications
- MBA or advanced degree.
- Experience with account-based marketing (ABM).
- Familiarity with Salesforce.
- Prior experience in SaaS or technology companies.
